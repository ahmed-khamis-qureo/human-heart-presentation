The Human Heart

By: mr.obieda

Location and Size

The human heart is a vital muscular organ that functions as the central component of the circulatory system. It continuously pumps blood throughout the body, delivering oxygen and nutrients to tissues while removing carbon dioxide and other waste products. This powerful organ works tirelessly, beating approximately 100,000 times per day, ensuring that all body systems receive the support they need to function properly.

Blood Circulation Pathway

The heart plays a key role in the double circulatory system, which includes the pulmonary and systemic circuits. Blood flows from the body into the right side of the heart, then to the lungs for oxygenation. It then returns to the left side of the heart and is pumped out to the rest of the body. This continuous loop ensures that oxygen-rich blood reaches all tissues.
